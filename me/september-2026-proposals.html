The shape of it

The fifth night costs €150–336 a head

Adding Monday night to the Tuesday–Saturday plan works on every route. Marbella is the bargain: its Monday fare is €132 cheaper than the Tuesday one, so the extra night costs €160 all-in. NAP

Vico Equense, Bay of Naples

Naples · Italy · Cheapest

The option where not having a car is an advantage rather than a compromise. Vico Equense sits on the Circumvesuviana railway between Naples and Sorrento: Pompeii is twenty minutes one way, Sorrento fifteen the other, and the Sorrento ferry terminal opens up Capri and the Amalfi coast.

Five nights gives you room for all of it — Capri, Pompeii, Sorrento and Positano without ever repeating yourself.

Who it suits

A group that wants to do things — an island, a Roman city, four or five towns — and treats the hotel as somewhere to sleep and swim between them.

Main trade-offThe fifth night is expensive here: Eden Bleu jumps €910 for one extra night, far more than a fifth of the four-night rate. And the 10:05 Saturday flight means an early last morning.

Getting around without a car

Naples → Vico Equense · ~45 min

From arrivals, the Curreri coach runs direct to Sorrento (about €12 a head) and you double back one stop on the train; a private minivan straight to the hotel is simpler with four people and luggage. After that the Circumvesuviana railway does everything — Pompeii, Sorrento, Naples — and ferries leave Sorrento for Capri and Positano through the day.

Estimated cost: €180 return for airport transfers, plus about €200 across five days of trains and ferries. Estimate — confirm

The five nights

Arrival through departure
  • Mon 14Down to the bay

    Land 16:00, transfer along the coast, checked in around six. A first walk through Vico Equense — a working Italian town rather than a resort — and dinner.

  • Tue 15Capri

    Train to Sorrento, ferry across. The Blue Grotto if the sea is flat, the chairlift up Monte Solaro if it isn't, and a long lunch in Anacapri before the late boat back.

  • Wed 16Pompeii, then nothing

    Twenty-five minutes on the train, three hours in the ruins before the heat peaks. Back for the pool and an early dinner.

  • Thu 17Sorrento

    The cliff-top town properly: Villa Comunale, the Marina Grande for fish, the ceramic shops, and a sunset drink looking at Vesuvius.

  • Fri 18Positano by sea

    Ferry down the Amalfi coast — the only sensible way to see it, and the day where not having a car is straightforwardly better than having one.

  • Sat 19Early doors

    Up around half seven, transfer to Naples for the 10:05. Home at 12:45 with the whole weekend still in front of you.

AGP

Marbella, Costa del Sol

Málaga · Spain · Best Value

The standout on these dates. Monday's fare is cheaper than Tuesday's — €949 against €1,081 — so you gain a whole night and save €132 on the flights at the same time. Nowhere else does that.

It also gets the shape right: you land at 10:20 on Monday and don't fly home until 19:10 on Saturday, so five nights buys five genuinely full days. Two Superior rooms with terraces, walking distance to both the old town and the beach, with free cancellation.

Who it suits

A group that wants beach, town and pool all reachable on foot, and values long days at both ends over shaving a few euro off the fare.

Main trade-offThe longest transfer of the Spanish options at forty minutes, and breakfast isn't included — which is why the food line here is €1,300.

Getting around without a car

AGP → Marbella · 55 km · ~40 min

Book a private transfer rather than joining the taxi queue — roughly €75 each way for a van, forty minutes down the AP-7. Once there the old town, the beach and the promenade are all on foot, Puerto Banús is a €15–25 taxi, and Ronda runs as a coach day trip with hotel pickup.

Estimated cost: €150 return for private transfers, plus about €100 across five days of local taxis. Estimate — confirm

The five nights

Arrival through departure
  • Mon 14On the sand by lunch

    Land 10:20, transfer down the coast, checked in around noon. The beach for the whole afternoon and a first dinner in the old town.

  • Tue 15Nothing at all

    Pool in the morning, beach after lunch, an evening walk along the promenade. The day the trip justifies itself.

  • Wed 16Ronda

    A coach day trip inland: the Puente Nuevo over the gorge, the old bullring, a bodega lunch. Collected from and returned to the hotel.

  • Thu 17Old town and the marina

    Marbella's Casco Antiguo on foot — orange trees, whitewash, lunch on Calle Ancha — then a taxi to Puerto Banús for the boats and the people-watching.

  • Fri 18Beach club

    A full day on the sand with a long lunch, or the spa if the weather turns. Last dinner somewhere in town.

  • Sat 19A genuinely full last day

    The flight isn't until 19:10. Late checkout, bags with reception, a final long afternoon by the sea before the 16:00 transfer. Home at 22:10.

TFS

Costa Adeje, Tenerife

Tenerife South · Canary Islands · Bougie Pick

The only genuine five-star that fits, and the best-reviewed hotel here: 9.2 across 2,294 reviews, with a spa, a serious breakfast and colonial-style gardens above the Costa Adeje seafront.

The fifth night costs almost nothing on this route — the Monday fare is only €20 more than the Tuesday one, and the hotel adds €289. Flown by TUI, so checked bags are in the fare rather than bolted on afterwards.

Who it suits

A group that wants the hotel to be the holiday, guaranteed September heat, and a twenty-minute transfer from the runway to a sun lounger.

Main trade-offThe longest flight at five hours, an evening arrival, and a return that lands at 00:15 on Sunday morning. You get your Sunday, but you'll sleep through the first half of it.

Getting around without a car

TFS → Costa Adeje · 20 km · ~20 min

A taxi from Tenerife South runs about €40 for the four of you and takes twenty minutes up the TF-1 — the shortest transfer here. Once there you genuinely don't need anything: the promenade connects the beaches, restaurants and Puerto Colón, and Teide and whale-watching trips both run coaches that collect from Costa Adeje hotels.

Estimated cost: €80 return by taxi, plus about €120 across five days of local taxis. Estimate — confirm

The five nights

Arrival through departure
  • Mon 14Late in, straight to dinner

    Land 19:05, taxi twenty minutes, checked in before eight. A drink in the gardens, dinner at the hotel, early night.

  • Tue 15Duque and back

    Playa del Duque in the morning, pool in the afternoon. Walk the coastal path to La Caleta for fish at sunset.

  • Wed 16Teide

    A coach excursion up to the national park — cable car to 3,555 m if the wind allows, then the caldera floor. Collected from and returned to the hotel.

  • Thu 17Whales, then the spa

    Catamaran from Puerto Colón, ten minutes along the front — resident pilot whales and bottlenose dolphins year-round. Spa and pool for the afternoon.

  • Fri 18Nowhere

    Gardens, pool, beach, a long lunch. The evening on the promenade at Puerto Colón.

  • Sat 19Borrowed hours

    Late checkout and the pool until mid-afternoon, taxi at half four for the 18:30. Home at 00:15 on Sunday.

Before you book

  • You land at 00:15 on Sunday. Still home before Monday, but plan how you get back from Schiphol after midnight — and don't schedule anything for Sunday morning.
  • Confirm the room category. Booking quoted €2,600 for two rooms with breakfast and free cancellation, but the property page listed higher-tier Triple Superior rooms at more. The €2,600 is real; pin down exactly which room it buys.
  • Bags are included — but verify the allowance. The TUI fare showed no cabin-bag-only restriction, which normally means hold luggage is in. Confirm the weight limit at booking rather than at the airport.
  • Evening arrival. Landing at 19:05 means the first day is dinner only.
  • Tenerife North was checked and has no nonstop service from Amsterdam, so Tenerife South is the only way in.
PMI

Palma, Mallorca

Palma · Balearic Islands · Most Relaxing

The easiest travelling of the four: two and a half hours each way, a fifteen-minute taxi at the far end, in at 09:30 on Monday and home at 22:30 on Saturday. Both ends of the trip are full days, which no other option manages alongside a flight this short.

Moving to Monday is what buys that — Tuesday's only easyJet outbound leaves at 16:15 and lands at 18:40. Here you're at the rooftop pool by eleven on day one.

Who it suits

A group that gets restless in a resort, wants a city to walk out into in the evening, and would rather spend the travel time on a lounger.

Main trade-offIt's the one option that misses your budget — €1,524, or €24 over — and the two rooms are twins rather than doubles. A single Quadruple Room brings it to €1,413 but puts all four of you in one room.

Getting around without a car

PMI → Palma · 8 km · ~15 min

The shortest airport run of the four — fifteen minutes by taxi, roughly €35 for a van with four people and bags. After that the city does the work: the old town is walkable, the Sóller vintage train leaves from Plaça d'Espanya in the centre, and buses run along the bay to the better beaches at Illetas and Cala Major.

Estimated cost: €70 return by taxi, plus about €100 across five days of buses and local taxis. Estimate — confirm

The five nights

Arrival through departure
  • Mon 14Pool by eleven

    Land 09:30, taxi fifteen minutes, checked in around eleven. The rooftop pool for the afternoon, then a first walk along the Paseo Marítimo into Santa Catalina for dinner.

  • Tue 15The old town

    La Seu cathedral in the morning light, the Arab baths, Bellver Castle above the city. Lunch at the Mercat de Santa Catalina, afternoon back on the roof.

  • Wed 16Sóller by wooden train

    The 1912 train from Plaça d'Espanya through the Tramuntana orange groves, then the open-sided tram down to Port de Sóller for lunch by the water. No car, all day.

  • Thu 17Beach day

    Bus or taxi fifteen minutes west to Illetas — small coves, clear water, a beach club if you want one. Back for sunset on the roof.

  • Fri 18Slow

    Pools in the morning, Es Baluard modern art museum in the afternoon if anyone's inclined, and a long tapas crawl through Santa Catalina to finish.

  • Sat 19A full last day

    Pools, late checkout, bags at reception, a final lunch by the water. Airport at six for the 19:55. Home at 22:30.

Before you book

  • This is the one option over budget, by €24 a head. It's inside the contingency line, but it isn't under €1,500 and I'm not going to round it there.
  • The fix, if you want one: a single Quadruple Room — 30 m², four single beds, two left — is €2,126 instead of €2,539, which brings the trip to €1,413 a head. Cheaper, but all four of you share one room.
  • Twin beds either way. Four singles across two rooms on the headline option. Spanish twins can usually be joined into doubles, but request it rather than assume it.
  • Breakfast costs extra at €15 a head — about €300 across the group for five mornings.
  • easyJet fares are cabin-bag only. €260 baggage estimate at roughly €32 per bag per leg.
  • City beaches are ordinary. If lying on sand all day is the point, Marbella or Tenerife will serve you better.
